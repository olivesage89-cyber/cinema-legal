Common questions

How do I identify a movie or show?

Point your camera at what's playing and scan, let Cinema listen to the audio, or tap ＋ to upload a clip. You can also share a video to Cinema from another app.

How do subscriptions and the free trial work?

Cinema offers a weekly and an annual plan, both with a 3-day free trial. Subscriptions are billed through your Apple Account and renew automatically until canceled. Manage or cancel anytime in Settings → Apple Account → Subscriptions, or from Cinema's Subscription screen.

How do I restore a purchase?

On the subscription screen tap "Restore purchases" (also available on the paywall). This re-checks your Apple Account for an active subscription.

How do I cancel?

Open Settings → Apple Account → Subscriptions on your device, choose Cinema, and cancel. You keep access until the end of the current billing period. Cinema's Subscription screen also links you straight there.

How do I delete my account?

In Cinema, go to Settings → Manage account → Delete account, or email us and we'll remove your account and data.

An identification was wrong or didn't work.

Clearer, longer clips identify best. If a result is wrong, try a different scene or upload the clip. Still stuck? Email us the details and we'll help.
