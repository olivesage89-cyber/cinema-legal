Privacy Policy

Cinema · Last updated: August 12, 2026

This policy explains what information Cinema ("we", "us", the "App") collects, why, and your choices. If you have questions, contact us at kingdaniel18000@gmail.com.

Cinema helps you identify movies and TV shows from a clip you scan with the camera, record audio from, or upload/share, and keeps a collection of what you've identified and saved.

Information we collect

  • Account information. When you sign in with Apple or Google, we receive your name and email address to create and secure your account. With Sign in with Apple you may choose to hide your email, in which case we receive a private relay address.
  • Your collection and saved titles. The titles you identify and save are stored so your list is available across your devices. This is linked to your account.
  • Clips you submit for identification. When you scan, record, or upload a clip, the video or audio is sent to our identification service (including a third-party AI model) to recognize the title. Clips are used only to identify the title and are not used for advertising or sold to anyone.
  • Subscription status. Purchases are processed by Apple. We receive your subscription status from Apple to unlock features. We do not receive or store your payment card details.

Camera, microphone, and photo access

Cinema requests camera and microphone access only when you actively use the identify feature, and photo/video access only when you choose a video to upload. These are used solely to identify a movie or show. We do not access them in the background.

How we use information

  • To provide the core feature — identifying titles from your clips.
  • To save and sync your collection and preferences.
  • To provide and manage your subscription.
  • To secure your account and prevent abuse.

Third-party services

  • Apple — Sign in with Apple and subscription billing (App Store / StoreKit).
  • Google — Google sign-in, if you choose it.
  • Our backend provider — we use Supabase to host your account and saved titles.
  • TMDB — movie and TV metadata and images are provided via The Movie Database API. This product uses the TMDB API but is not endorsed or certified by TMDB.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it to track you across other companies' apps or websites.

Data retention & deletion

We keep your account and saved titles until you delete your account. You can delete your account and associated data from within the app (Settings → Manage account → Delete account) or by emailing kingdaniel18000@gmail.com. Subscription records held by Apple are governed by Apple's policies.

Children

Cinema is not directed to children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your region), and we do not knowingly collect their personal information.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, or delete your personal information. Contact us at kingdaniel18000@gmail.com and we'll respond as required by applicable law.

Changes

We may update this policy; we'll revise the date above and, for material changes, provide notice in the app.
