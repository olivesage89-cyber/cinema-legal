Pick your video style

Three kinds of video you can make. Cozy, quiet and real, filmed vertically on your phone.

Option 1

Going to bed

Winding down for the night, getting cozy under soft lights and a warm blanket, then relaxing with a movie.

Option 2

Movie night snacks

Making hot chocolate and popcorn for an at-home movie night, then watching together on the couch.

Option 3

Night routine

Doing cute night routine things: skincare, a face mask, tidying your space and a little snack, then getting cozy in bed with a movie.

Don't save Cinema for the very end. People scroll fast, so show the app while they're still watching: a short full-screen clip of you typing a mood and tapping a movie, partway through your video.

Quick tips

  • Record your screen from Control Center
  • Soft, trending sound under the video
  • Keep notifications off while recording
  • Don't show other apps' logos

The simple rules

  • Post on your own account: TikTok, Instagram Reels or YouTube Shorts. Posting the same video on all three counts once.
  • Label it as an ad with the platform's "Paid partnership" or "Ad" setting.
  • Show the app: type a real mood into Cinema and show the movies it finds (see How to make your video).
  • Views are counted 7 days after posting. Send a screenshot of your stats and we pay within 3 days. Milestones don't add up: you get the highest one your video reaches.
  • We can repost your video on Cinema's accounts and use it in ads, with credit to you.

Questions

Do I need a lot of followers?

No. Small accounts often get great views on TikTok and Reels. The Base + Milestone plan pays $20 per video no matter your size.

Do I need to pay for the app?

No. We'll give you full access for free once you're accepted.

What should the video look like?

Cozy and real, like a movie night with friends, a partner or on your own. Show yourself typing a mood into Cinema and the movies it finds. We send hooks and ideas with your brief.

Can I switch plans?

Yes. Tell us after any video and we'll move you to another plan for the next one.
