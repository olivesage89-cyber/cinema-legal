How it works

  1. Apply

    Pick a pay plan and send your application. About 2 minutes.

  2. Get your brief

    We email video ideas and hooks within 2 days. Say it in your own words.

  3. Film and post

    Post on your own TikTok, Reels or Shorts. Show yourself typing a mood into Cinema partway through.

  4. Send your stats

    After 7 days, send a screenshot of your views.

  5. Get paid

    We pay within 3 days by PayPal, Venmo, Cash App or Zelle.

Video styles

Cozy, quiet and real.

Three kinds of video you can make, filmed vertically on your phone.

Option 1

Going to bed

Winding down for the night, getting cozy under soft lights and a warm blanket, then relaxing with a movie.

Option 2

Movie night snacks

Making hot chocolate and popcorn for an at-home movie night, then watching together on the couch.

Option 3

Night routine

Doing cute night routine things: skincare, a face mask, tidying your space and a little snack, then getting cozy in bed with a movie.

Don't save Cinema for the very end. People scroll fast, so show it while they're still watching: film yourself using the app on your phone, typing a mood and tapping a movie, partway through your video.

Cozy moment ideas

  • Making hot chocolate or popcorn
  • Evening skincare or face mask
  • Rainy window, candles, blanket
  • Date night or a night with friends

Quick tips

  • Turn your screen brightness up so the app shows on camera
  • Soft, trending sound under the video
  • Keep notifications off while recording
  • Don't show other apps' logos

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Do I need a lot of followers?

No. Small accounts often get great views on TikTok and Reels. The Base + Milestone plan pays $20 per video no matter your size.

Do I need to pay for the app?

No. We'll give you full access for free once you're accepted.

What should the video look like?

Cozy and real, like a movie night with friends, a partner or on your own. Show yourself typing a mood into Cinema and the movies it finds. We send hooks and ideas with your brief.

When do I get paid?

Views are counted 7 days after you post. Send us a screenshot of your stats and we pay within 3 days.

Can I switch plans?

Yes. Tell us after any video and we'll move you to another plan for the next one.
